# PS5 Save Mounter Batch

Back up individual saves, all saves from a game, or all game saves from your PS5 to your PC.

1. **[Download the release](https://github.com/Piece-OK/PS5-Save-Mounter-Batch/releases).** It works on Windows 64-bit. No separate .NET installation is needed.
2. **Extract the ZIP and open `PS5 Save Mounter Batch.exe`.** Keep `savemounter.elf` in the same folder.
3. **Connect to your jailbroken PS5.** Start elfldr on the PS5, enter its IP address in the app, and click **Connect**. The app sends savemounter.elf automatically.
4. **Set your preferences.** Choose a console user, click **Load game library**, choose your download folder, and select the games or saves you want.
5. **Click Dump selected saves.** Your backups are saved in the app's `Backups` folder unless you chose another location.
